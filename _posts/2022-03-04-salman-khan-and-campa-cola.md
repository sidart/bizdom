Long before Sooraj Barjatya cast him in *Maine Pyar Kiya* (1989), Salman Khan faced the camera for an advertisement for Campa Cola.

The story goes back to Bombay's Sea Rock Hotel, where Salman, in an attempt to impress a young woman in a red sari, swam the entire length of the swimming pool underwater. The woman happened to be Aarti Gupta, who would subsequently marry advertising filmmaker Kailash Surendranath.

Kailash was looking for models who could swim underwater for a Campa Cola commercial. Aarti recommended Salman, and that is how he got his first advertising assignment.

The commercial, dated 1983, was shot in the Andaman Islands and also featured Ayesha Shroff (Tiger Shroff's mother), Aarti Gupta, Sunil Nischol, Vanessa Vaz and Shiraz Merchant. According to Kailash, it was India's first underwater advertisement, shot using a makeshift underwater camera rig.

Campa Cola had emerged as one of India's popular soft drink brands following Coca-Cola's departure from the country in 1977.

Incidentally, the music for the commercial was composed by Ronnie Desai and Shiv Mathur.

Kailash himself uploaded the [original Campa Cola advertisement](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3o7GT3Jcnfs) on YouTube in August 2018.
