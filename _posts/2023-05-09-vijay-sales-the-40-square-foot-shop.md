In 1954, Nanu Gupta moved from Haryana to Bombay and began working in the distribution business of Usha International, which primarily dealt in sewing machines and electric fans. After spending nearly 13 years in the trade, Gupta decided to establish his own business.

In 1967, he borrowed Rs 2,000 from family and friends to open a small shop in Mahim, naming it Vijay Sales after his younger brother, who was also his business partner. The shop, reportedly measuring just 40 sq ft, was so small that Gupta had to fold up his chair whenever customers walked in.

With limited working capital, Gupta procured goods directly from manufacturers on credit, paying them once the goods were sold. By eliminating intermediaries, he could offer better prices than many competing retailers.

By the early 1970s, Gupta had added black-and-white televisions to his inventory. The Mahim shop became a local landmark, identified by its large illuminated Bharat TV signboard. When Bharat TV ceased operations, Gupta realised the commercial disadvantage of having his shop identified with a manufacturer's brand and began promoting the Vijay Sales name independently.

The introduction of colour television during the 1982 Asian Games considerably expanded the market for television retailers. However, Gupta waited until 1986 to open his second outlet, a 600 sq ft showroom in Bandra. His elder son, Nilesh, joined the business in 1992.

The subsequent entry of Korean consumer electronics brands, particularly Samsung, LG and Daewoo, increased the space requirements of retailers. Larger refrigerators, washing machines and televisions needed considerably more display space than the products Gupta had originally sold.

Vijay Sales remained largely a Mumbai-based business for nearly four decades. Its geographical expansion accelerated after 2007, with stores coming up in Gujarat, Pune and Delhi.

In April 2019, the Guptas made their first-ever acquisition, buying Hyderabad-based TMC Electronics from the Nangunuri family. The transaction gave Vijay Sales an additional 19 outlets across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.
