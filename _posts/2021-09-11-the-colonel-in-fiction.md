Haruki Murakami’s novel *Kafka on the Shore*, the Oscar-winning French animated film *Logorama*, a romance novella titled *Tender Wings of Desire* and a series of DC Comics have one character in common: Colonel Sanders, the founder of Kentucky Fried Chicken (KFC).

Sanders, with his trademark white suit, black string tie and goatee, became the face of the restaurant chain. Even after selling his business in 1964, he continued to appear in its advertisements and promotional campaigns.

In Murakami’s 2002 novel, Colonel Sanders appears as a supernatural character who borrows the appearance of a familiar commercial icon.

KFC itself has experimented with placing its founder in fictional settings. In 2015, the company partnered with DC’s Custom Creative Studio to produce *The Colonel of Two Worlds*, a comic book in which Sanders encounters his evil counterpart, Colonel Sunder, from an alternate universe called Earth-3. Two more comic books followed, featuring characters from the DC universe.

In 2017, to coincide with Mother’s Day, KFC released *Tender Wings of Desire*, a Victorian-era romance novella featuring Harland Sanders as the romantic interest.

Two years later, KFC ventured into the world of video games with *I Love You, Colonel Sanders!*, a dating simulator in which players could attempt to win the Colonel’s affection.
