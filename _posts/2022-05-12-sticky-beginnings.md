The origins of Pidilite Industries can be traced to 1954, when Balvantray Kalyanji Parekh, along with his brother Sushil, started a business called Parekh Dyechem Industries. The brothers initially traded in dyes and subsequently ventured into manufacturing pigment emulsions used in textile printing.

In those days, Indian carpenters largely depended on animal-based glue, which had to be heated before use. In 1959, the Parekh brothers introduced Fevicol, a white synthetic resin adhesive that could be used without such preparation. They adopted a rather unusual marketing practice for the time, approaching carpenters directly instead of relying primarily on retailers.

In 1963, the company established its first modern manufacturing plant at Kondivita village in Bombay, the premises which would later house Pidilite's corporate headquarters.

Over the years, helped by its memorable advertising campaigns, Fevicol entered the vocabulary of ordinary Indians. By 2012, it had even found its way into a Bollywood song, *Fevicol Se*, in the film Dabangg 2.
