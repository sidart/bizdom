Shyam Sundar Goenka moved from Calcutta to Bangalore to establish a business supplying machinery and raw materials to textile manufacturers. As the business grew, maintaining accounts manually became increasingly difficult. Goenka looked for an accounting software package but could not find one that was simple enough for his requirements.

He turned to his son Bharat Goenka, a mathematics graduate, and asked him to develop a program that could be used by a businessman with little knowledge of computers. The father-son duo established Peutronics in 1986.

Among the early products developed by Bharat was an accounting package called The Accountant. In those days, operating business software often required users to learn specific computer commands. Bharat designed his software around the accounting practices that Indian businessmen and their munims were already familiar with.

Peutronics was subsequently renamed Tally Solutions in 1999. Over the following decades, Tally expanded its capabilities beyond bookkeeping to include inventory management, payroll and taxation. The introduction of VAT and subsequently GST created further demand for its products among small and medium enterprises.

In 2021, Tally Solutions applied to the Reserve Bank of India for a small finance bank licence, seeking to enter the banking business that served much of its existing software customer base.
