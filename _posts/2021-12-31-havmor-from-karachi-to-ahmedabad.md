Satish Chandra Chona started his ice cream business in Karachi in 1944, naming it Havmor, a play on the words ‘Have More’. Barely three years later, following the Partition of India, Chona had to abandon the business he had built and migrate to India.

He initially settled in Dehradun and subsequently moved to Indore, where his attempts to revive the ice cream business met with little success. In 1951, Chona moved to Ahmedabad and started afresh, selling ice cream from a handcart near the city's railway station.

Havmor gradually became a popular name in Gujarat. Over the following decades, successive generations of the Chona family expanded the business beyond ice cream, establishing restaurants and developing a distribution network across western India.

In November 2017, South Korea's Lotte Confectionery announced the acquisition of Havmor's ice cream business for ₹1,020 crore. The Chona family retained its restaurant business.

A brand born in undivided India had, after seven decades, become part of a South Korean confectionery group.
