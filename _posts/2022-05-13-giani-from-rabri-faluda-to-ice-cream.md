Following Partition, Giani Gurcharan Singh, a sweetmeat vendor from Lyallpur (now Faisalabad in Pakistan), migrated to Delhi with his family. He initially sold sweets from a handcart before establishing Giani di Hatti in 1956 at Fatehpuri, Chandni Chowk. The word Giani means ‘the learned’ in Punjabi.

The shop became particularly famous for its rabri faluda, mango shakes and gajar ka halwa. Among its regular patrons were politicians and film personalities, including Raj Kapoor and Mohammed Rafi. In 1962, the family reportedly spent Rs 12,000 on a second-hand machine, a substantial investment for the small establishment.

Gurcharan Singh's eldest son, Gurbachan Singh, joined the family business in 1970 while still an undergraduate. Another branch of the family recalls introducing three varieties of homemade ice cream in 1978: vanilla, strawberry and butterscotch.

The third generation ventured into franchising, taking the business beyond Old Delhi. Gurbachan's son Taranjit Singh, who studied frozen dessert making in Italy, introduced several new products, including a rather amusing sundae called ‘Kuch Nahi’, meant for customers accompanying their families who insisted they wanted nothing to eat.

Incidentally, Giani Ice Cream and Giani's Ice Cream, despite their nearly identical names and shared origins at Fatehpuri, are operated by different branches of the original family.
