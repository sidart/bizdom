In 1962, the American singer Bobby Darin, already popular for his numbers *Mack the Knife* and *Beyond the Sea*, won a Golden Globe for his performance in *Come September* (1961). During the shooting of the film in Italy, Darin fell in love with his co-star Sandra Dee, and the two got married even before the film was released.

The film revolves around an American businessman, played by Rock Hudson, who visits his Italian villa every September, unaware that his caretaker runs it as a hotel during his absence.

The storyline found its way into Shakti Samanta's *Kashmir Ki Kali* (1964), starring our very own Yahoo man, Shammi Kapoor, and Sharmila Tagore. The same premise was used in *Mere Sanam* (1965) and the Tamil film *Anbe Vaa* (1966), starring M.G. Ramachandran.

Bobby Darin had also composed the signature tune of *Come September*, which found an audience of its own in India. Suman Kalyanpur recorded a Hindi adaptation under the music direction of Shankar-Jaikishan.

In 1995, two Hindi films borrowed the same tune. Anu Malik used it in *Dole Dole Dil Dole* in *Baazi*, while Nadeem-Shravan used it for *Nazrein Mili Dil Dhadka* in *Raja*.

One can also hear strains of the tune in *Meri Jaan A, Meri Jaan B* from Kamal Swaroop's rather unconventional film *Om-Dar-B-Dar* (1988). Anurag Kashyap later acknowledged this sequence as an inspiration for the brass-band version of *Emotional Atyachar* in *Dev.D* (2009).

Incidentally, Darin became increasingly involved in American politics during the 1960s, campaigned for Robert F. Kennedy and was present at the Ambassador Hotel in Los Angeles when Kennedy was assassinated in 1968.

Darin himself died in 1973, aged just 37.
