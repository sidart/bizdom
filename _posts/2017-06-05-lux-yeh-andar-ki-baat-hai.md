In 1957, Girdharilal Todi established Biswanath Hosiery Mills in Calcutta to manufacture innerwear. In its early years, much of the production was outsourced. The Todis would procure yarn and get workers to manufacture vests and briefs according to their specifications. Over the years, the family brought more of the manufacturing process under its own control.

The arrival of satellite television in India in the early 1990s gave the Todis an opportunity to take their brand, Lux, to a national audience. Until then, the company had been advertising sporadically.

Ashok Todi credits B.R. Chopra, the producer of the popular television serial *Mahabharat*, with giving Lux its famous advertising line. During a conversation with the Todi brothers, Chopra asked them what they manufactured. On being told that they were in the innerwear business, he remarked, ‘Yeh toh andar ki baat hai.’ The expression became the brand's tagline.

In 1992, the company roped in Sunny Deol, then among Bollywood's popular action heroes, for its television commercial. The rather cheeky double meaning of the line made it easy for people to remember and use in everyday conversations.

The company began exporting its products in 1993 and organised a conference for its hosiery dealers the following year. Biswanath Hosiery Mills was subsequently reorganised under Lux Industries in 1995.

Interestingly, while Lux went on to associate itself with several Bollywood stars, including Salman Khan and Shah Rukh Khan, its most enduring contribution to Indian advertising remains a casual remark made by a filmmaker.
